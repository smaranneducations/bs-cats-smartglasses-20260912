## Feedback synchronization result

Twenty-eight major requirements are now centralized in `config/requirements-traceability.json`. Each records its authority, status, implementation evidence, runtime evidence, remaining gap and next closure action. The charter now requires this registry to be updated for every substantive steer.

This does not mean every conversational sentence becomes permanent policy. Feedback is classified as a correction, scoped preference, reusable rule, ontology proposal, evaluation case, experiment or justified no-change decision. Credentials and unnecessary personal data are excluded.

# Business model and measurement

## Goal and hard boundary

Create sustained, ethical and lawful contribution from a reusable domain intelligence asset while keeping operator effort and risk low. Ethics, legality, safety, truth, privacy, rights and human dignity override revenue. Revenue is measured after variable model cost, cloud cost, media cost, payment fees, refunds, support, moderation, taxes where applicable, and attributed human time.

## Seed runway

The cumulative experiment ceiling is USD 500, including the owner-reported USD 80 subscription. USD 450 is the stop-admitting threshold and USD 50 is reserved for delayed or fixed charges. If the ceiling is reached, no new paid work begins. The operating executive presents achieved assets, collected revenue, outstanding liabilities, unit economics and options to the financial owner. More seed money requires an explicit new approval and is not expected by default.

## Candidate revenue channels

| Channel | Value sold | Earliest proof | Main gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube advertising | Useful original short-form audience attention | Eligible channel and retained viewing | Platform eligibility and content quality |
| Affiliate or referral | Qualified product intent | Approved program plus attributed outbound action | Eligibility, disclosure and editorial independence |
| Sponsorship | Clearly disclosed access to an audience | Qualified sponsor inquiry | Contract, disclosure and factual independence |
| Paid report or tool | Decision-ready analysis | Landing-page intent or paid pilot | Buyer problem and maintained evidence |
| Dataset or API | Governed current domain knowledge | Qualified data-buyer inquiry | Rights, freshness, service levels and privacy |
| Customer-funded generation | Governed short-form video assembled from approved inputs | Paid pilot with measured unit economics | Abuse, rights, quality, payment and margin |
| Platform license | Reusable engine and operating method | Qualified solo-founder or publisher pilot | Productization and support burden |
| Education and supervised training | Practical learning with the platform | Paid cohort or apprenticeship experiment | Labor, education, tax and consumer-law review |
| Asset or channel sale | Maintained knowledge, software and audience asset | Credible buyer inquiry | Ownership, transferability and valuation evidence |

## Customer-funded generation

The initial hypothesis is a 30 percent gross platform margin over measured direct job cost. The customer price must reserve model tokens, image generation, rendering, storage, moderation, retries, payment fees, refunds, and support. The platform does not claim zero cost simply because the customer funds model use.

One customer artifact remains 9:16 and 30 to 90 seconds. Longer narratives are composed as separately reviewable canonical clips rather than an unbounded render. Customer input never bypasses ethics, source, rights, safety, identity, or publication policy.

## Success scorecard

| Dimension | Core measures |
| --- | --- |
| Delivery | Accepted end-to-end releases, failure recovery, release cycle time |
| Trust | Correction rate, unresolved high-impact conflicts, evidence and rights coverage |
| Audience | Qualified views, completion, return rate, evidence opens, useful comments |
| Intent | Outbound clicks, report requests, API inquiries, sponsor inquiries |
| Economics | Collected revenue, contribution, cost per accepted artifact, support minutes |
| Compounding asset | Current assertions, reusable media, evaluated primitives, ontology reuse |
| Human workload | Administrator minutes, exception count, repeated manual actions |

Views alone do not establish demand. Gross revenue alone does not establish a viable business. The system scales only after contribution, quality, repeat engagement, and operator workload meet an explicit experiment threshold.
